How It Comes Together

The Process

01

Discovery

Understanding the brand's world before drawing a single element — audience, ambition, and the gap between how it's perceived today and how it deserves to be seen.

02

Strategy

Positioning, voice, and the point of view the brand will hold — the foundation every visual decision gets measured against.

03

Creative Direction

Moodboards, palette, and typography direction — the world the brand will live inside, agreed before a single asset is finalized.

04

Design

Identity, packaging, and digital presence, built out in full — considered, refined, and tested against the strategy set at the start.

05

Launch

Final assets delivered, the presence goes live — ready to represent the business at full strength from day one.

No. 006 — Selected Experience

Who thinks this way?

Point of View

The studio

Meridian Haus was founded on a simple belief: a brand's presence should never undersell what it actually offers. Too many boutique businesses and small brands settle for looking small because that's what's available at their budget — templated, generic, forgettable.

Every project starts the same way — by asking what makes this brand's world unlike anyone else's, and building outward from there. The result is work that reads as considered rather than convenient, whether it's a lookbook, an identity system, or a full digital presence.

Based in Cardiff-by-the-Sea, working with clients wherever they are.
