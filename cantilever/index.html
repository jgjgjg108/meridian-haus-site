Approach

How the studio works.

"A building should be legible from its section before it's legible from its facade."

Cantilever starts every project on the site, not at the desk — reading slope, light, and wind before drawing a single wall. The goal isn't a signature look; it's a building that could only sit where it sits.

Materials are chosen for how they age, not how they render. Concrete is left to weather. Steel is left to oxidize where it's safe to. Nothing is finished to hide what it's made of.
