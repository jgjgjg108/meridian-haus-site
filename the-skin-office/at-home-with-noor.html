At Home with Noor · October 2026

The must-haves on my counter this month

A monthly look at what our founder, Noor Haddad, actually uses at home

People assume an esthetician’s bathroom is full of serums. Mine mostly isn’t. Most of what my skin needs happens in the treatment room; at home I keep it short, and I keep the things that make the routine feel like a ritual rather than a chore.

Here is what is on my counter this month. Nothing here is sponsored, and none of it is sold in the studio. These are just the things I keep buying again.

Chanel

N°5 Eau de Parfum

I don’t wear fragrance in the treatment room, so this is the first thing I put on when I get home. Two sprays on the wrists, never on the face.

Shop at chanel.com
Aesop

Resurrection Aromatique Hand Wash

Mandarin rind, rosemary and cedar. I wash my hands forty times a day, and this is the one that doesn’t leave them tight.

Shop at aesop.com
Summer Fridays

Lip Butter Balm, Brown Sugar

A sheer brown tint that looks like your lips, only better rested. One lives by the sink, one in every coat pocket.

Shop at summerfridays.com
Jenny Bird

Sees Ring

Rings come off for every facial, so the ones I keep have to earn their spot on the counter. This sculptural silver one always goes back on.

Shop at jenny-bird.com
