About

One room, one esthetician, your skin.

Founder portrait1200 × 1500 — coming soon

The Skin Office started as one treatment room above a bakery on Bedford Avenue, and one idea: that a facial should leave you understanding your skin better than when you walked in.

Noor Haddad trained as an esthetician in Los Angeles and spent a decade in busy medical spas before opening the studio in 2021. She missed knowing her clients’ skin over years rather than minutes, so the studio was built for exactly that: you see the same person every time, and every visit is written up.

The room is stocked with a handful of professional formulas, chosen for what is in them rather than who makes them. If we can’t explain an ingredient, we don’t use it.

How we work

Three house rules

01

Look first.

Every appointment starts with a close look under good light. We treat the skin in front of us, not the one on the booking form.

02

Less, consistently.

A short routine you actually follow beats a long one you don’t. We’ll often tell you to stop using something.

03

Write it down.

You leave with notes on what we did, what we saw and what to do at home, so the next visit picks up where this one ended.
