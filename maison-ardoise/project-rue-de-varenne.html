Paris, 7th arrondissement

Rue de Varenne

The apartment had been divided, painted and re-divided for a century. Our work began with subtraction: removing partitions, stripping six layers of paint from the boiserie, and letting the original enfilade run uninterrupted from salon to dining room.

Against that restored shell we set a deliberately quiet collection — a long oak table, black-lacquered chairs, linen at the windows and a single bronze chandelier. Nothing competes with the light that moves along the herringbone each afternoon.
