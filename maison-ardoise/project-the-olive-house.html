The Olive House

The clients asked for colour but not noise. We built the palette from a single bolt of olive wool: deepened in the bedroom, softened in the living room, and carried into a green-veined marble basin in the bath.

Oak joinery ties the floors together, from the bedside tables to the kitchen. Linen, wool and bouclé add texture so the rooms read as layered rather than decorated.
