Laight Street Loft

The brief was quiet: a home that feels like a gallery between exhibitions. We washed the walls in warm lime plaster, poured a pale mineral floor throughout, and hung unlined linen at full height so the light arrives softened.

Against that calm we placed a few strong pieces — a vintage black leather sofa, a raw-edged travertine side table, an ebonised oak console — and divided the rooms with steel-framed reeded glass, so space and light flow through without the noise.
