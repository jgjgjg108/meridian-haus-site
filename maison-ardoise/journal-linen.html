Dressing a Bed in Linen, Season by Season

A linen bed is never quite made, and that is its charm. The fibre is cool in July, warm in January, and it softens with every wash until it drapes like old cotton. Here is how we layer it through the year.

Summer

A single fitted sheet, a flat sheet folded back over a lightweight linen coverlet, and two sleeping pillows. Keep colour pale — oat, chalk, flax — and leave the decorative cushions in the cupboard.

Autumn

Add a wool blanket across the foot of the bed and a pair of euro shams in a deeper tone. This is where we bring in olive or tobacco: one colour, repeated twice.

Layer in odd numbers and keep to three tones. The bed will look considered, never styled.

Winter

Swap the coverlet for a down duvet in a heavyweight linen cover, and fold a bouclé throw at the corner. The texture contrast — crisp, nubby, smooth — does more than any pattern.

Whatever the season, wash linen cool, shake it out and dry it on the line when you can. Iron only the pillowcases, if anything. The crumple is the luxury.
