Stone Ridge House

Working alongside the architect from the first sketches, we treated the house as a sequence of thresholds: arched openings, a sculpted plaster stair, and a wall of oak-framed glass that dissolves into the garden.

Every surface is mineral or timber. Lime plaster was hand-troweled on site in three tones of warm white; oak was oiled, never lacquered, so it deepens with the seasons. Furniture is low and generous, so the eye goes out to the garden.
