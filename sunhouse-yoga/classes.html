Classes

Nine classes, one shape.

Two daily rhythms, a six-part series that travels from root to crown, and a reset every Sunday. Every one of them opens with a mantra.

Morning & nightThe Chakra SeriesFull Reset
Every class

Mantra, breath, asana, meditation.

01

Mantra

Every class opens with one. A seed sound or a short chant that marks the start and tunes the room.

02

Breath

Pranayama before we move: a technique chosen for that class, from a calming long exhale to breath of fire.

03

Asana

The physical practice. It looks different in every class, from Iyengar precision to a candlelit yin hold.

04

Meditation

We finish seated, with a mudra, a hand gesture that gives the mind somewhere to rest.
