Every class, four parts

The same shape, every time.

Whether it is a 6:30 am sunrise flow or a candlelit Deep Flow, every Sunhouse class has the same four parts. You always know where you are.

01

Mantra

Every class opens with one. A seed sound or a short chant that marks the start and tunes the room.

02

Breath

Pranayama before we move: a technique chosen for that class, from a calming long exhale to breath of fire.

03

Asana

The physical practice. It looks different in every class, from Iyengar precision to a candlelit yin hold.

04

Meditation

We finish seated, with a mudra, a hand gesture that gives the mind somewhere to rest.

The house

An old citrus-packing office, turned toward the light.

Sunhouse sits on Signal Street in a 1920s building with tall west windows. The afternoon sun crosses the whole floor, which is why the evening classes start with the blinds up.

We keep the room small, the classes to eighteen mats and the teachers to five people we trust.
