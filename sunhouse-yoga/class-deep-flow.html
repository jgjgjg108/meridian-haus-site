Deep Flow

Yin & vinyasa by candlelight

The lights go off and the candles come on. Half a slow, low vinyasa, half long yin holds, so you leave ready for sleep.

How it unfolds

Four parts, in order.

Deep Flow class at Sunhousephoto
  1. 01
    Mantra

    Om Shanti Shanti Shanti

    Peace, said three times: for the body, the mind and the day that is ending.

  2. 02
    Breath

    Chandra Bhedana

    Moon breath through the left nostril, cooling and calming before bed.

  3. 03
    Asana

    Slow flow, then yin

    Thirty minutes of fluid, low-to-the-ground vinyasa, then three-to-five-minute holds for hips, spine and shoulders.

  4. 04
    Meditation

    Dhyana mudra

    Hands resting in the lap, right palm in left, thumbs touching. A body-scan meditation in candlelight.
