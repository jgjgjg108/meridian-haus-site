The daily rhythm

Sunrise Salutations

Surya Namaskar

The doors open while it is still blue outside. By the end of class the sun is in the room. A steady, rhythmic vinyasa built on rounds of Surya Namaskar.

How it unfolds

Four parts, in order.

  1. 01
    Mantra

    Om Suryaya Namaha

    A greeting to the sun, chanted three times as the room gets lighter.

  2. 02
    Breath

    Ujjayi

    Ocean breath, slow and audible, to set the tempo for every movement that follows.

  3. 03
    Asana

    Sun salutations A and B

    Rounds that build in pace and length, with standing balances and a long hold in warrior two.

  4. 04
    Meditation

    Surya mudra

    Ring finger folded under the thumb. A short seated meditation on warmth and clear intention for the day.
