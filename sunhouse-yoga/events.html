Inversions Workshop

Getting upside down, safely
1:00 pm – 3:30 pmWith Theo$55

Two and a half hours on getting upside down with confidence. We break headstand, forearm stand and handstand into their parts, practise each at the wall, and spend time on coming down as well as going up.

How it flows

  1. Opening mantra and a short breath practice
  2. Shoulder and core preparation
  3. Headstand and forearm stand, step by step at the wall
  4. Handstand drills for those who want them
  5. Counter-poses and a mudra meditation

All levels. You do not need to be able to do any inversion yet.

Body Reset

Twists and agni
1:00 pm – 3:00 pmWith Maren$45

A two-hour practice of twists and heat-building work to stoke agni, the digestive fire. Good after a heavy week, a holiday, or a change of season.

How it flows

  1. Opening mantra: RAM
  2. Kapalabhati and belly breathing
  3. Twisting sequence, standing, seated and reclined
  4. Core and heat-building flow
  5. Seated meditation with Rudra mudra

All levels. Come with an empty-ish stomach: nothing heavy for two hours before.

Myofascial Release & Lymphatic Drainage Yoga

Rolling, releasing, draining
1:00 pm – 3:00 pmWith Ines$45

A slow workshop combining self-massage with therapy balls, gentle movement and inversions that help lymph drain. Expect to leave lighter and a little sleepy.

How it flows

  1. Opening mantra and diaphragmatic breathing
  2. Ball work for feet, hips, back and neck
  3. Gentle flow that follows the lymph pathways
  4. Legs up the wall and supported inversions
  5. Meditation with Prithvi mudra

All levels. Balls and props provided.

Yoga Foundations

Philosophy, breath and sound
3:00 pm – 5:30 pmWith Leila & Dev$55

A flow class with a talk inside it. While you move, Leila guides a meditation on one idea from yoga philosophy: the yamas, the koshas, the eight limbs. Then Dev leads breathwork and a sound healing with bowls and gong.

How it flows

  1. Opening mantra
  2. Flow with a guided philosophy meditation
  3. Breathwork
  4. Sound healing with bowls and gong
  5. Closing mudra meditation

Everyone, especially if you are new and want to know what the words mean.
