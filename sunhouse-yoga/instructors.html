Five people we trust

Small on purpose. Every Sunhouse teacher leads the same four-part class, mantra to mudra, in their own way.

Leila AmariInstructor photo · 1600 × 2000
Founder

Leila Amari

Teaches like a slow exhale: steady, warm, and very clear about why.

Leila found yoga at nineteen in a community hall in Lisbon and has practised every day since. She trained in hatha in Mysore, Kriya in Rishikesh and kundalini in New Mexico, then taught in Los Angeles for ten years before opening Sunhouse.

Teaches

  • RootMonday, Thursday
  • CrownSaturday
  • Full ResetSunday
  • Yoga FoundationsMonthly
Maren HoltInstructor photo · 1600 × 2000
Vinyasa

Maren Holt

Will get you warm by 6:45 am.

A former college rower, Maren came to yoga for her back and stayed for the breath. She teaches strong, musical vinyasa and builds the kind of playlists people ask for afterwards.

Teaches

  • Sunrise SalutationsMonday, Wednesday
  • Solar PlexusWednesday, Friday
  • Body ResetMonthly
Ines CarvalhoInstructor photo · 1600 × 2000
Iyengar

Ines Carvalho

Precise in the morning, fluid at night.

Ines trained for six years in the Iyengar method in Lisbon and Pune. She knows exactly which block, strap or wall will make a pose make sense, and then on Tuesday night she turns the lights down for Sacral.

Teaches

  • SacralTuesday
  • ThroatTuesday
  • Myofascial & LymphaticMonthly
Theo MarshInstructor photo · 1600 × 2000
Yin & inversions

Theo Marsh

His Deep Flow candles are lit at 7:55 sharp.

Theo spent a decade as a circus acrobat before training in yin and inversions in Vancouver. He is patient, funny and very good at getting people upside down for the first time.

Teaches

  • Deep FlowTuesday, Thursday
  • AjnaThursday
  • Inversions WorkshopMonthly
Dev RamanInstructor photo · 1600 × 2000
Breath & flow

Dev Raman

Breath first, then everything else.

Dev is a pranayama teacher and sound healer who studied breath in Chennai and gong in London. He looks after the studio’s twenty-two singing bowls and the gong named Agnes.

Teaches

  • Sunrise SalutationsFriday, Saturday
  • Deep FlowSunday
  • Yoga FoundationsMonthly
