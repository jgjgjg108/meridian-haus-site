Ajna

The third eye

The sixth chakra sits between the brows and is about insight and seeing clearly. The best way we know to see differently is to turn upside down.

How it unfolds

Four parts, in order.

  1. 01
    Mantra

    OM

    The seed sound of the third eye, chanted three times, then felt in silence.

  2. 02
    Breath

    Nadi Shodhana

    Alternate-nostril breathing to balance the two sides of the body and focus attention.

  3. 03
    Asana

    Inversions

    Dolphin, headstand and forearm-stand practice at the wall, with plenty of options for every level.

  4. 04
    Meditation

    Kalesvara mudra

    Middle fingertips and thumbs touching, other fingers folded. A gaze meditation on a single point.
