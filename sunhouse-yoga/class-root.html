Root

Muladhara

The first chakra lives at the base of the spine and is about safety and steadiness. A slow hatha class with plenty of breathwork, built to bring a busy nervous system back down.

How it unfolds

Four parts, in order.

Root class at Sunhousephoto
  1. 01
    Mantra

    LAM

    The seed sound of the root chakra, chanted low and long so you can feel it in the floor.

  2. 02
    Breath

    Extended exhale

    Breathing in for four and out for eight, the simplest way to ask the nervous system to settle.

  3. 03
    Asana

    Grounding hatha

    Long-held standing poses, wide-legged folds, squats and supported bridges. Feet and legs, slowly.

  4. 04
    Meditation

    Prithvi mudra

    Ring finger to thumb, the earth seal. A meditation on weight, contact and being held.
