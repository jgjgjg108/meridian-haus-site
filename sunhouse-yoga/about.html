About

Built around the light

Sunhouse is a small yoga studio in Ojai, founded in 2019 by Leila Amari. Five teachers, nine classes, one room facing west.

Our founder

Leila Amari

Leila found yoga at nineteen in a community hall in Lisbon and has practised every day since. She trained in hatha in Mysore, in Kriya in Rishikesh and in kundalini in New Mexico, and taught for ten years in Los Angeles before moving north.

She opened Sunhouse in a former citrus-packing office because of the light. The studio is built around the rhythm of the day: a salutation at sunrise, a candlelit class at night, and a full reset every Sunday.

The Chakra Series is her favourite thing she has made: six classes, six teachers’ styles, one thread from root to crown.

“I wanted a room where you can feel the time of day. The practice changes with it.”

Leila Amari, founder
What we believe

Three things

01

Every class has a shape.

Mantra, breath, asana, meditation. The same four parts every time, so you can stop wondering what comes next and just practise.

02

Small on purpose.

Eighteen mats, five teachers and a short schedule. Teachers learn your name and your knees.

03

Tradition, explained.

We chant, use mudras and talk about chakras, and we always say what something is and why we are doing it.

The room

27 Signal Street

A 1920s building with tall west windows, old fir floors and a ceiling high enough for handstands. Paper lanterns, linen blinds, a shelf of singing bowls and a gong named Agnes.

There is a small tea corner by the door. Stay after class.
