Full Reset

Kundalini, every Sunday

Once a week we reset everything. A kundalini class that works through the body from root to crown with kriyas, breath and sound, and ends lying down under the gong.

How it unfolds

Four parts, in order.

  1. 01
    Mantra

    Ong Namo Guru Dev Namo

    The Adi mantra, chanted three times to open every kundalini class.

  2. 02
    Breath

    Breath of fire

    Rapid, even breathing through the nose, followed by long, deep breaths to settle.

  3. 03
    Asana

    A kriya

    A set sequence of movements, holds and breath, different each week, often with the eyes closed.

  4. 04
    Meditation

    Gyan mudra

    Index finger to thumb. A chanted meditation, then gong relaxation.
