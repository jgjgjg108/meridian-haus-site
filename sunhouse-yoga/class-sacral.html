The Chakra Series · 02 of 06

Sacral

Svadhisthana

The second chakra is water: emotion, pleasure, creativity. Lights low, music on, and a fluid, circular flow that spends most of its time in the hips.

How it unfolds

Four parts, in order.

  1. 01
    Mantra

    VAM

    The seed sound of the sacral chakra, chanted softly while rocking the pelvis.

  2. 02
    Breath

    Wave breath

    Breath that fills from the belly up and empties from the top down, like a wave coming in and going out.

  3. 03
    Asana

    Fluid hip flow

    Circles, spirals, lunges and low squats. Few straight lines and no counting.

  4. 04
    Meditation

    Shakti mudra

    Ring and little fingers touching, the others folded. A meditation on the breath moving like water.
